Questions.

Where do the prices come from?

We collect public listings from several resale marketplaces and show the lowest price next to each seller.

Do you sell the phones?

No. We compare prices and send you to the seller, so you always buy directly from them.

What do the condition labels mean?

Like new means almost no wear. Good means light marks. Fair means visible wear but fully working.

How fresh are the prices?

Prices are refreshed regularly, so what you see is close to live.
